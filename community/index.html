Local history resources

  • Martinsville-Henry County Historical Society

    Runs the Heritage Center & Museum in the historic Henry County Courthouse and hosts talks and programs on local history.

  • Col. George Waller Chapter, Sons of the American Revolution

    The local chapter of the Virginia Society SAR, serving the Martinsville-Henry County area.
