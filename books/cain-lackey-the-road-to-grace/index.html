About the book

Cain Lackey: The Road to Grace tells the life and legacy of an unlikely man of God. The book draws on primary sources, church records, oral histories, and published works.

About the author

Andrew S. Doss is a local historian from the Martinsville and Henry County area. He shares Virginia history on the YouTube channel History In Sight.
