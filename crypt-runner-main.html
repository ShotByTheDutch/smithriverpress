HOW TO PLAY

Goal

  • Crypt: find the glowing portal on each level and descend. The portal stays sealed (it glows red) until every monster on the level is dead, including the ones hiding behind signs, and from round 5 that includes the boss. You keep your weapons, ammo and armor between levels.
  • Game kills are the monsters placed on the map. Secret kills are the ones hiding behind ambush signs. Both counters sit at the bottom right, with a count of the secret rooms you've found under them.

Monsters

  • Imp (round 1) rushes you in melee. Brute (round 2) is slow and tough. Caster (round 3) keeps its distance and throws fireballs. Madstone (round 4 on) glows, then fires a ring of light bolts in every direction. Slip between them or duck behind a wall. It drops a big health pack.
  • Some monsters hide behind signs. Walk toward an ambush sign and it may sink into the floor and release one.

Bosses (crypt round 5 and up)

  • Mirror King reverses your left and right controls. The Blackout turns the lights nearly off. The Prism warps and recolors the view. Molasses Golem slows you down. Their orbs do damage and apply the effect, so dodge them.
  • PlasmaPonger bolts lose most of their punch against bosses from far away or around corners, so use other weapons at range. Round 11 is the grand finale: all four bosses, each sealed in its own room. Only one boss door can be open at a time; defeat that boss before opening the next. Defeat them all to clear the crypt.

Weapons (keys 1 to 9 and 0)

  • 1 Fists: free, close range, silent. 2 Rifle: your standard gun. Shots hit harder up close and weaker from far away. 3 Double barrel: 2 ammo a shot and a huge point-blank hit. 4 Chain gun: very fast, drains ammo.
  • 5 PlasmaPonger: fast bolts that bounce off walls up to three times. A direct hit is the killer. Bounced hits are weak, and a bolt coming back can graze you for a point or two. 6 Rocket launcher: splash damage to monsters, and to you if you fire at something close. 7 Flamethrower: short range, sets monsters on fire. 8 Railgun: one beam through every monster in line. 9 Lightning gun: short zaps that arc to a second monster. 0 Annihilator: a huge blast with big splash, so keep your distance.
  • Weapons 3 to 0 are found in secret rooms behind signs, one new weapon per map, in order.
  • Shots go exactly where the crosshair points, up and down as well. Tilt the view to hit things high or low. Shots that land on a wall, floor or ceiling throw sparks.

Pickups and wall features

  • Red cross is health, a gold-edged cross is big health, yellow crates are ammo, and glowing guns are weapons.
  • Blue armor panel on a wall: walk into it to equip armor. It soaks up 60% of each hit while it lasts. You can only grab it when your armor is below full.
  • Purple glowing panels teleport you to another one.
  • Sealed doors: the room with the portal is shut behind a gloomy door. Shoot the small activation panel beside it, with any weapon and a steady aim, to open it. Each door has one panel outside and one inside, and shooting either one opens that door and lights both. Monsters inside don't show on the minimap until a door opens.
  • Signs: most are just decoration. A sign with a brass knob, a slight tilt and dark seams is a secret door. Touch it and it sinks, revealing a secret room. Every map hides two: one with health, one with the next new weapon and ammo. Other signs may be ambushes.

Controls

  • Desktop: WASD or the arrow keys to move and sidestep, mouse or Q and E to turn, mouse to look up or down, click or Space to fire, 1 to 0 to change weapon, P to pause, M to mute, PgUp and PgDn to tilt the view, Home to level it. The Controls screen lets you remap keys.
  • Phone: hold the top of the left half of the screen to walk forward and the bottom to walk back. Drag on the right half to turn and look up or down. Use the FIRE, SWAP, GHOST and PAUSE buttons.
