About Smith River Press

Two history lovers helping regional authors get their books made.

How we started

Smith River Press was started by two history enthusiasts who spend their free time digging through records, courthouse files, and family stories from Martinsville, Henry County, and the Blue Ridge foothills around them. Along the way we kept meeting people with great ideas for books but nowhere to take them.

Why we exist

Larger publishers often pass on regional non-fiction. A county history or a study of one family’s farm is a hard sell to a national audience, even when it matters a great deal to the people who live here. We think those books deserve to be published well.

What we do

We help authors in the Smith River region and the surrounding areas find quality publishing resources and options for non-fiction. That means pointing you toward the people and services that make a good book, and talking through the choices so you can pick the route that suits your project and your budget.

Who we’re for

Local historians, genealogists, researchers, and storytellers writing non-fiction about this part of Virginia and the places nearby. You don’t need an agent or a finished manuscript to start a conversation.

Beyond books

We also build free tools for local history. The Henry County Patriot Database lets anyone search the 1777 Oaths of Allegiance and Revolutionary War service rolls. More are on the way, and you can find them under Apps & Tools.

Our community

We’re part of a wider circle of people working to keep local history alive. See our other projects, along with friends and organizations we recommend, on the Community page.

Get in touch

Have a manuscript, an idea, or a question about publishing? Write to us and tell us about your project.
